One line, 200 characters at most, in English: the elevator pitch a city pigeon gives to investors for its startup.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes one line (163 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`: a city
pigeon pitching PigeonPost, crumb-delivery subscriptions — lunch on
your ledge before you even know you're hungry, seed round open,
worms accepted as payment.
